Overview

Purpose

To communicate and manage all necessary information regarding white water rafting including but not limited to time of events, legal documents, sign up sheets, and what to bring

Audience

Primarily middle aged to younger aged thrill seekers who want to go white water rafting but don't have a group, are new to the area, or don't have the necessary supplies and training.
